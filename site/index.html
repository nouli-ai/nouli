What is Nouli?

Nouli is a ledger-backed decision network that records AI decisions and connects them to the facts and earlier answers they use.

Use the terminal app, CLI, or Rust library to ask yes/no, choice, and score questions.

Branch histories to explore alternatives. Blend them to bring earlier decisions together.

Give it
a turn.

  1. Install.
    $brew install nouli-ai/tap/nouli

    On Linux, use the download installer.

  2. Connect a model. Configure a compatible decision service. In the app, Ctrl-L or /model opens the picker and connection setup.
    $nouli connect
  3. Open a session. Enter adds Context rows; blank Enter moves to Question. Send with Enter on a nonempty Question or Ctrl-S:
    $nouli open travel
