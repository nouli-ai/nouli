Nouli is a multi-turn decision harness. It runs decision models over many turns: each turn adds facts, and the model decides again with the whole history in view.

A session keeps every fact, question and answer. Add a turn and the model decides again with all of it in view. Branch to try something else. Blend to put separate histories together.
