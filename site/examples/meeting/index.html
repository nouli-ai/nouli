Examples / Simple

A meeting trip

One session, two travel options, and one changed fact. Start with a choice, check a yes/no question, then revise the plan using the same options.

Configure a provider, then open nouli open travel. Type plain text in the form. These screens replay the saved Jev 1.14 answers from the home-page travel recording; your live answers and probabilities may differ.

For the shell version, choose a fresh session prefix:

example=meeting
show the steps as

Add context, press Tab for the question, use Shift-Tab to choose the answer type, then enter options and send. Use Form, Answer and Decisions below to inspect the captured screens.

01Choose the cheaper trip

Put the travel facts in Context. Press Tab, write the question, and use Shift-Tab for Choose one. Enter the two options on separate rows. Enter adds another option; a second consecutive Enter or Ctrl-S sends. 

 nouli 0.4.0 · travel
travel › The train costs 40 USD and arrives 30 minutes before the meeting. A
taxi costs 70 USD and also arrives in time. Prefer the cheaper option that
arrives on time.
travel › How should I travel to the meeting?
