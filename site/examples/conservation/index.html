Examples

Energy conservation planning

The initial recorded plan meets the sample 250 kW target. After the battery reading changes, the model incorrectly retains that offer. Steps 7, 9, and 10 explain why the recorded output violates the reserve constraint.

The problem

A facility wants to reduce grid imports during a conservation request while preserving critical refrigeration and a battery reserve. It can discharge a battery and defer vehicle charging. A corrected energy reading arrives before the plan is executed, so the available battery contribution must be reconsidered.

The historical context is the Cal OES September 6, 2022 emergency conservation alert, issued at 17:45 and requesting reduced nonessential use until 21:00 where health permits. The facility, readings, reserve, operating constraints, and 250 kW target are illustrative. Stored energy is already AC-deliverable; conversion losses are accounted for.

What we will do

We will keep the external conservation request, battery telemetry, and facility obligations separate. Two blends will choose battery and flexible-load contributions, then a final blend will combine them. We will try an unsaved what-if, record corrected telemetry, and save a revised plan.

Before you start

Configure a provider and model, then run the commands in order in the same shell. Nouli reads credentials from your shell or the .env in your current directory. Each decision calls your configured provider and may incur charges.

The outputs below were captured with typesafe/jev-1.14.0. They are a recorded run, not guaranteed results. Probabilities, session IDs, and model answers may differ when you run the commands.

Set a session prefix for this walkthrough. Change it before repeating the example so the new sessions have unique names:

example=energy
show the steps as

Open nouli open request. Add each step’s context, question and answers in the form, then press Enter to send. Captures replay the recorded model answers in the current TUI.
