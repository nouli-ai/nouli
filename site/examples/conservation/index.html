Examples

Energy conservation planning

The initial recorded plan meets the sample 250 kW target. After the battery reading changes, the model incorrectly retains that offer. Steps 7, 9, and 10 explain why the recorded output violates the reserve constraint.

The problem

A facility wants to reduce grid imports during a conservation request while preserving critical refrigeration and a battery reserve. It can discharge a battery and defer vehicle charging. A corrected energy reading arrives before the plan is executed, so the available battery contribution must be reconsidered.

The historical context is the Cal OES September 6, 2022 emergency conservation alert, issued at 17:45 and requesting reduced nonessential use until 21:00 where health permits. The facility, readings, reserve, operating constraints, and 250 kW target are illustrative. Stored energy is already AC-deliverable; conversion losses are accounted for.

What we will do

We will keep the external conservation request, battery telemetry, and facility obligations separate. Two blends will choose battery and flexible-load contributions, then a final blend will combine them. We will try an unsaved what-if, record corrected telemetry, and save a revised plan.

Before you start

Configure a provider and model, then run the commands in order in the same shell. Nouli reads credentials from your shell or the .env in your current directory. Each decision calls your configured provider and may incur charges.

The outputs below were captured with typesafe/jev-1.14.0. They are a recorded run, not guaranteed results. Probabilities, session IDs, and model answers may differ when you run the commands.

Set a session prefix for this walkthrough. Change it before repeating the example so the new sessions have unique names:

example=energy

01Record the conservation request

Record the external request separately from the facility limits. The two-hour planning interval is 18:00-20:00, after the historical alert.

nouli --format text new "$example-request" \
  'Cal OES reported a September 6, 2022 emergency alert at 17:45, requesting
reductions in nonessential electricity use where health permits until 21:00.
Our illustrative facility is considering 18:00-20:00. This is a voluntary
conservation request, not a market dispatch instruction.' \
  -q 'request: Which type of response fits this request? [Reduce nonessential use while preserving critical operations,
    Include critical operations in load reductions,
    Request clarification before choosing any response]'

07Try an unsaved alternative

Use whatif to evaluate a hypothetical 700 kWh reading without saving a new decision or updating the telemetry session. A session keeps asking its questions, so --drop plan leaves the plan question out of this one turn.

nouli --format text whatif "$example-plan" \
  'What-if only: replace the stored-energy reading with 700 kWh for this
evaluation. Keep the 500 kWh reserve, 200 kW power limit, 100 kW deferrable
charging, and two-hour interval. Choose the largest feasible offer toward the
250 kW goal. No plan has executed.' \
  -q 'what_if: Which conservation plan is feasible for 18:00-20:00? [Reduce imports by 250 kW: 150 kW battery plus 100 kW deferred charging,
    Reduce imports by 200 kW: 100 kW battery plus 100 kW deferred charging; report a 50 kW shortfall,
    Request a revised plan because neither offer fits the constraints]' \
  --drop plan

Review finding: the model keeps the full 250 kW offer. With 700 kWh stored and a 500 kWh reserve, only 200 kWh can be used over two hours, allowing 100 kW from the battery. The feasible combined offer is 200 kW, with a 50 kW shortfall. The captured what-if answer is incorrect.

08Record corrected telemetry

Continue the telemetry session with the corrected 700 kWh reading. Unlike the what-if, this becomes saved source evidence for new decisions. The same question ID replaces the session's standing question with the reworded one.

nouli --format text add "$example-battery" \
  'Confirmed illustrative correction at 17:55, replacing the 17:50 reading:
stored AC-deliverable energy is 700 kWh. The power limit remains 200 kW. No
discharge has occurred; this is a corrected measurement before the interval
starts.' \
