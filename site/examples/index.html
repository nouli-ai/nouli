Examples

Worked examples of Nouli, the multi-turn decision harness. Each walkthrough builds decisions in separate sessions, blends them, and revisits the result when new evidence arrives. Outputs come from recorded runs with TypeSafe's Jev 1.14.0.

Read a walkthrough as a worked example, or copy its commands into your terminal in order. Each one starts with a session prefix; change it before running the walkthrough again. Configure a provider first. Calls use your provider account and may cost money.

Your answers and probabilities may differ from the recorded ones. The loan and energy walkthroughs include incorrect model answers and show the calculations that expose them. A decision graph records history; it doesn't certify the answers. The commands produce recommendations only: they don't move a robot, approve credit, commit capital, send recall notices, patch equipment, or operate a battery.
