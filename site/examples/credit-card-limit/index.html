Credit card limit increase

The recorded recommendation changes from a 6000 USD line to a 10000 USD line after new income verification. The initial capacity answer has a selected probability of 0.43, which is worth examining even though it selects the policy-consistent option.

The problem

A customer requests a total credit line of 10000 USD, up from 5000 USD. The bank can instead offer 6000 USD. The existing account has a strong payment record, but the larger line also needs a payment-capacity review. New verified income arrives before any limit is changed.

CFPB Regulation Z section 1026.51 describes considering income or assets and current obligations before a credit card limit increase. The account, thresholds, and stressed payment estimates below are fictional. They demonstrate selected review steps, not a complete credit or compliance process.

What we will do

We will create independent histories for cash flow, account performance, and review policy. Two blends will assess payment capacity and account standing. A final blend will recommend a limit. We will then continue the income history and create a revised capacity review and recommendation.

Before you start

Configure a provider and model, then run the commands in order in the same shell. Nouli reads credentials from your shell or the .env in your current directory. Each decision calls your configured provider and may incur charges.

The outputs below were captured with typesafe/jev-1.14.0. They are a recorded run, not guaranteed results. Probabilities, session IDs, and model answers may differ when you run the commands.

Set a session prefix for this walkthrough. Change it before repeating the example so the new sessions have unique names:

example=card

01Record income and existing obligations

Record income, living expenses, and other debt payments. Exclude the existing payment on this card because the capacity review will substitute a stressed payment for the candidate total line.

nouli --format text --name "$example-cashflow" \
  --state 'Fictional card application C-202 for an adult over 21, verification at 09:00:
recurring net monthly income is 4000 USD, essential living expenses are 2500 USD,
and payments on other debts are 700 USD. The 700 USD excludes payments on this
credit card. There are no other obligations. All records required by the
sample policy are verified.' \
  --questions '[
  {
    "id": "cashflow",
    "prompt": "What monthly amount is available before this card payment?",
    "answer_space": {
      "type": "choice",
      "options": [
        {"id": "s800", "description": "800 USD"},
        {"id": "s1500", "description": "1500 USD"},
        {"id": "missing", "description": "Request missing income or obligation records"}
      ]
    }
  }
]'

06Combine the reviews into a limit recommendation

  --from "$example-standing" \
  --name "$example-limit" \
  --state 'Combine the capacity and account-standing reviews. Recommend the highest
eligible total line up to the requested 10000 USD only when both gates pass. A
passing account review cannot override insufficient payment capacity. If the

standing passes but no increase qualifies, retain 5000 USD only if its capacity
test passes; otherwise refer for manual review. This recommendation does not change the account.' \
  --questions '[
