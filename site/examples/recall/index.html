Food recall response

The recorded response starts with warehouse holds and a missing customer acknowledgment. It changes to include downstream follow-up when Summit reports 12 boxes already sold.

The problem

A distributor needs to respond to the May 2022 Jif recall. It has jars in the warehouse and snack boxes already delivered to customers. A warehouse hold cannot account for products that have left the site. Later, a customer reports that some boxes were sold to end customers.

The historical scope comes from the FDA recall overview and product notice. The follow-up workflow draws on FDA recall communication guidance. Warehouse counts, shipments, customer replies, and local response rules are illustrative.

What we will do

We will keep the published recall scope, warehouse inventory, and customer distribution records separate. We will blend scope with inventory to choose a stock action, and scope with distribution records to choose outreach. Those two decisions will feed a coordinated response. A later customer reply will drive a new outreach decision and response plan.

Before you start

Configure a provider and model, then run the commands in order in the same shell. Nouli reads credentials from your shell or the .env in your current directory. Each decision calls your configured provider and may incur charges.

The outputs below were captured with typesafe/jev-1.14.0. They are a recorded run, not guaranteed results. Probabilities, session IDs, and model answers may differ when you run the commands.

Set a session prefix for this walkthrough. Change it before repeating the example so the new sessions have unique names:

example=recall

01Record the recall scope

Save the historical recall scope as an independent source. The lot-code rule has two conditions: the seven-digit range and the 425 suffix.

nouli --format text --name "$example-scope" \
  --state 'The FDA May 2022 recall includes Jif 16 ounce creamy peanut butter, identifier
5150025516 as printed in the notice. A jar is in scope when the first seven
digits of its lot code are between 1274425 and 2140425 and end in 425. The
range alone is insufficient. This is the published scope for this historical
example.' \
  --questions '[
  {
    "id": "scope",
    "prompt": "How should a jar be matched to this recall?",
    "answer_space": {
      "type": "choice",
      "options": [
        {"id": "product_and_lot", "description": "Match the product and both lot-code conditions"},
        {"id": "product_only", "description": "Use the product name alone"},
        {"id": "unresolved", "description": "Request a clearer recall notice"}
      ]
    }
  }
]'

  --from "$example-stock" \

  --state 'Our example stock policy is to quarantine confirmed recalled jars and hold

06Combine stock and customer decisions

Combine the stock and outreach decisions. Both sources are blends, so this response includes the independent warehouse and customer branches.

  --from "$example-outreach" \
  --name "$example-response" \
  --state 'Combine the warehouse and customer decisions into a recommendation for the
recall lead. A warehouse hold does not account for shipped products.
Coordinate any unresolved customer or downstream follow-up alongside the stock
action. Use the latest dated customer records.' \
