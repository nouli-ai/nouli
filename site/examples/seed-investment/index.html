Examples

Seed investment decision

A seed fund reviews an applied-AI company, plans diligence, and drafts a recommendation for its investment committee. Customer reference calls then change the biggest risk, and the recommendation changes with it.

The problem

A partner at a seed fund is evaluating Tidewell, an AI agent that prepares and follows up dental insurance claims. The partner has notes on the team and the traction, a fund policy on check size, ownership, and valuation, and a committee meeting on Monday. The evidence arrives during the week: reference calls land after the first recommendation is drafted.

A good process shows what each recommendation was based on, and updates it when the evidence changes without losing the earlier reasoning. That's what a committee memo, a partner review, or a post-mortem two years later needs.

Tidewell, its founders, its metrics, and the fund policy are fictional.

What we will do

We keep team, traction, and fund policy in separate sessions. We blend team and traction to plan diligence, then blend the plan with the fund policy to draft the recommendation. When the reference calls come in, we add them to the traction session and rebuild the diligence plan and the recommendation from the updated history. Finally, we try a repriced round without saving it.

Before you start

Configure a provider and model, then run the commands in order in the same shell. Nouli reads credentials from your shell or the .env in your current directory. Each decision calls your configured provider and may cost money.

The outputs below were recorded with typesafe/jev-1.14.0. Your probabilities, session IDs, and answers may differ.

Set a session prefix for this walkthrough. Change it before running the walkthrough again, so the new sessions get unique names:

example=seed
show the steps as

Open nouli open team. Add each step’s context, question and answers in the form, then press Enter to send. Captures replay the recorded model answers in the current TUI.

09Try new terms without saving

Try a repriced round from the updated recommendation. whatif runs it from the updated recommendation without saving anything. It asks only its own question, so the saved recommendation keeps its answer.

This unsaved alternative uses the shell command:

nouli --format text whatif "$example-ic-updated" \
  'What-if: the lead investor reprices the round to $3M at a $25M post-money valuation. Everything else is unchanged.' \
  -q 'terms: What should the partner do under these terms? [Keep the $1.2M recommendation,
    Ask for a smaller allocation at the new price,
    "Decline, because the new price is above the fund'\''s valuation limit"]'

What the graph keeps

The saved graph holds both recommendations side by side. The first is conditional on reference calls; the second is conditional on the bundling milestone. Each one traces back to the facts the model saw, and the fund policy feeds both without being copied. These commands make recommendations only; they don't commit capital.
